# Belgian mutuality overview — Mutuelle Exemple

> **FICTIONAL SAMPLE DATA.** Written for this portfolio project. Not an official publication of a Belgian mutualité / ziekenfonds, of MLOZ (Mutualités Libres / Onafhankelijke Ziekenfondsen), or of INAMI/RIZIV. Figures elsewhere in this folder are invented. Licence: MIT, same as this repository.

A Belgian mutualité (French) or ziekenfonds (Dutch) is the organisation through which residents access compulsory health insurance and, usually, an optional complementary cover. Mutuelle Exemple is not a real mutuality. It exists only so this service can demonstrate retrieval over a small bilingual corpus.

Compulsory cover reimburses care according to the legal tariff. The part of that tariff left to the member is often called the ticket modérateur (French) or remgeld (Dutch). A practitioner who is conventionné / geconventioneerd agrees to that tariff. Complementary cover is a separate contract; Mutuelle Exemple's complementary amounts are made up and appear in the French, Dutch, and PDF samples.

Nothing in this folder is legal, medical, or benefits advice.
